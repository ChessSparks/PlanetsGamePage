Planet 1 of 5

Kepler

Repair the ship

A meadow world with your ship wrecked in the grass. Solve line panels from the crash site to the ridge, drive a rover out to lay new cable, and climb a jump course to a panel high above the mushrooms.

Panel symbols Dots, breaks, sun and shade

NovaWe crashed. The ship is broken, and so am I.

Planet 2 of 5

Ambrosia

Restart the outpost

Lava, steam and a dead outpost. Bring the generator back to life, find answers cracked into the canyon floor at Dustbowl Depot, and pick up a blaster on your way to the crater lock.

Panel symbols Flames

NovaWhoever left these panels wanted them solved.

Planet 3 of 5

Orion

Cure the blight

A night world lit by its own forest. Carry the village's last ember as a lantern, bounce off giant glowcaps, and cure the infected locals with your blaster. It heals them, it doesn't hurt them.

Panel symbols Twin lights, readable only by your lantern

NovaOh, these ones bounce!

Planet 4 of 5

Ledara

Answer the distress signal

A frozen moon under an aurora. Stay warm, melt the ice, drive the rover across the snow, crack the lever puzzle and outrun the avalanche. Stop by Aurora Cove to meet the penguins.

Puzzle Levers, heat and ice

NovaPenguins. Of course there are penguins.

Planet 5 of 5

Veyra

Repair Nova

An old station under glass, and the end of the road. Wake its generator through the garden and the hangar, then face the last panel to bring Nova back.
